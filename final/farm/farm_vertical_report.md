# Commercial Farmer Lead-Generation Underwriting — Final First Pass

## Executive conclusion

**GO on commercial farmers, but enter through agricultural finance first.**

The best standalone niche is **farm equipment financing**, followed by **farm/ranch P&C insurance**. The more powerful account strategy is **Agricultural Finance** as one buyer market: equipment finance + land loans + operating credit.

Our conservative cleaned DataForSEO dataset shows roughly **13,590 monthly leadable searches** across those three finance intents at a blended CPC of about **$13.19**. These are comparative search signals, not unique people or TAM.

## The buyer strategy

Do not build a separate company for every keyword category. Build a farmer acquisition engine and sell standardized demand to sophisticated buyer classes.

**Agricultural finance** is the best first buyer class because the same lender relationship may monetize multiple farmer needs. Equipment finance also has direct evidence that lenders and brokers already purchase third-party leads.

## Why some huge farm markets failed

Seed/fertilizer, livestock feed and crop inputs are enormous farmer spend categories, but their buying motion is commonly captive to dealer, co-op, agronomist or territory relationships.

Drone spraying is the opposite problem: search demand is surprisingly strong, but service providers are too local and fragmented for the $10K-$100K/month buyer thesis.

Anaerobic digesters have huge tickets, but the qualifying-farm universe is finite and identifiable. That is better treated as account-based outbound than PPL.

Farm succession/life-insurance planning remains interesting outside search. It should be tested through direct mail, seminars, Meta and landowner-age/value data rather than Google Search.

## Method

Seven evaluation rounds were run. The first broad DataForSEO output was rejected. Later rounds introduced curated seeds, farm-specific relevance rules, native DataForSEO intent, semantic deduping, captive-brand separation, manual top-keyword auditing, and finally buyer-capacity gates.

Final score weights:
- Buyer capacity 25%
- Lead economics 15%
- Existing lead-buying culture 15%
- Clean measured search opportunity 15%
- Farmer/data addressability 10%
- Channel diversity 7.5%
- Geographic scalability 7.5%
- Low regulatory/operational friction 5%

A structural buyer problem can override the numerical score.

## Next step

Interview buyers before meaningful traffic spend. For each candidate validate:
- accepted lead definition
- minimum loan/project/premium size
- geography
- exclusive vs shared
- reject/return policy
- accepted CPL
- daily/monthly cap
- whether they can scale from $10K to $25K, $50K and eventually $100K/month

A real launch GO requires a buyer willing to define a paid pilot, not merely saying they are interested.
